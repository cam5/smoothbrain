# Use semicolons and em dashes throughout marketing copy

Marketing copy uses semicolons and em dashes throughout as a deliberate part of the house voice.

- Use semicolons to join closely related independent clauses rather than splitting them into separate short sentences.
- Use em dashes (—) for asides, emphasis, and pivots within a sentence.
- When editing or generating marketing copy, do not strip these out or "simplify" them into commas, periods, or hyphens.
- Use a true em dash character (—), not a hyphen (-) or double hyphen (--).
