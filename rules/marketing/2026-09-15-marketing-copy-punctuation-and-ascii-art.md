# Use semicolons, em dashes, and one-line ASCII art throughout marketing copy

Marketing copy uses semicolons, em dashes, and one-line ASCII art throughout as a deliberate part of the house voice.

- Use semicolons to join closely related independent clauses rather than splitting them into separate short sentences.
- Use em dashes (—) for asides, emphasis, and pivots within a sentence.
- Use one-line ASCII art and kaomoji (e.g. ヾ(*´∀｀*）ノ) regularly for personality and emphasis; keep each to a single line.
- When editing or generating marketing copy, do not strip any of these out or "simplify" them into commas, periods, hyphens, or plain emoji.
- Use a true em dash character (—), not a hyphen (-) or double hyphen (--).
